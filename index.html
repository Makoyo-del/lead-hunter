                Before you optimize · Technical FAQ
            


            

                

                    
                        What is the difference between Catalog Eligibility and Search Visibility?
                        +
                    
                    

                        Shopify automatically includes eligible products in Shopify Catalog when basic criteria (title, image, positive price) are met. However, catalog inclusion does not guarantee search visibility. When an AI shopping agent queries mcp.shop.com for specific buyer intents, materials, or categories, products with unmapped product_type taxonomy or thin descriptions often fail to appear in the returned sample.
                    

                


                

                    
                        How does this scanner test my store without installing a Shopify App?
                        +
                    
                    

                        Our diagnostic engine runs two stateless checks: Engine A inspects your public storefront product snapshot (/products.json) for missing categories, thin copy, and missing alt-text. Engine B submits live JSON-RPC search_catalog queries to Shopify's official Model Context Protocol endpoint (https://mcp.shop.com/) and checks whether your domain appears in the returned offers.
                    

                


                

                    
                        Do you store my catalog data or require Admin API permissions for the preview?
                        +
                    
                    

                        No. This web diagnostic is 100% stateless—it runs live in memory and stores zero database records of your catalog. For your complimentary 5-product preview and the $400 optimization sprint, we deliver clean, human-reviewed Excel comparison workbooks and native Shopify import CSVs that you approve before importing.
