Scoring Methodology

How Your Catalog Defect Score Is Calculated

The Catalog Defect Score (0–100) measures catalog completeness and search indexability across four programmatic criteria:

Thin Descriptions Listings with under 35 words lacking technical specifications or consumer benefits.
Missing Alt-Text Images with empty or missing alt text, preventing ranking in Google Image Search.
Missing Taxonomy Uncategorized product types that block automatic Google Shopping feed approval.
Unstructured Specs Descriptions lacking clean bulleted feature points, dimensions, or compatibility blocks.

Note: A catalog defect score reflects technical data gaps. Catalog completeness directly improves search indexability and on-page conversion clarity; we never claim unverified revenue projections.
